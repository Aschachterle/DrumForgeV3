About Extrudo

Extrudo is a parametric design platform for 3D printable projects. Our tools let you customize designs to your exact specifications, then generate print-ready STL files in seconds.

We started with electronic drums because we're drummers ourselves. Building custom e-drums shouldn't require CAD expertise or expensive software. With Extrudo, anyone can create professional-quality drum shells tailored to their needs.

All designs are optimized for home 3D printers using standard PLA or PETG filament. No special hardware required.
