🖨️ Nozzle Chase

Click when each letter finishes - just 7 clicks to win!

How to Play

  • Watch the nozzle draw each letter
  • When a letter is done, the screen glows green - CLICK!
  • You have 2 seconds to click after each letter
  • Don't click too early or you'll see "TOO EARLY!"
  • Miss 3 letters and it's game over
